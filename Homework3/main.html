The Pennsylvania Native Plant Initiative is dedicated to protecting and celebrating the native plant species that support Pennsylvania’s ecosystems, wildlife, and natural beauty. By promoting conservation, education, and responsible planting, the initiative helps preserve biodiversity and encourages healthier landscapes across the state.

The Pennsylvania Native Plant Initiative promotes the conservation and appreciation of native plants to protect Pennsylvania’s biodiversity, support healthy ecosystems, and inspire responsible stewardship of the natural landscape.

Featured Native Plants

Butterfly Weed (Asclepias tuberosa)

Asclepias tuberosa, commonly known as butterfly weed or pleurisy root, is a species of milkweed native to eastern and southwestern North America. It is commonly known as butterfly weed because of the butterflies that are attracted to the plant by its color and its copious production of nectar.
