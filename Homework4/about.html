About Us

Welcome to our Native Plant App! We are dedicated to providing information about native plants in your area. Our goal is to help you learn more about the plants that are native to your region and how to care for them. Our team of experts has curated a comprehensive database of native plants, including their characteristics, habitat, and care requirements.

We believe that by promoting the use of native plants, we can help preserve local ecosystems and support biodiversity. Native plants are adapted to the local climate and soil conditions, making them more resilient and easier to maintain than non-native species.
